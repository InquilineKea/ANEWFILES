Supercell band structure

Exact tight-binding diagonalisation of a commensurate twisted bilayer. The two kagome sheets are rotated by ∓θ/2 and solved together in one moiré unit cell, so every band shown is a true supercell eigenvalue, with no continuum approximation. Click a band to choose an energy, scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

Where the states live

Local density of states summed over eigenstates inside the amber energy window, at one moiré momentum. Dot area is the weight on each site. The background shading is the stacking registry: warm patches are AA-stacked, cool patches are AB or BA.

Twist sweep not run

For each commensurate angle up to the chosen limit, the solver samples five moiré momenta (Γ, K, M and two generic points). It reports how wide the descendant of the monolayer top band is, how narrow its narrowest sub-band gets, and how many gapped pieces it breaks into. Five momenta give lower bounds on widths and can miss band touchings, so treat this as a screen, not a measurement.
