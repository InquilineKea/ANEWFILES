Higher-order interactions

Interactions grouped by how many sites they couple: k = 2 are the pair bonds (J, J⊥), k ≥ 3 the hyperedges. Bars count the active interactions; dots give their energy per site. A k-body term −K∏s cannot be rewritten as a sum of pair terms, so a large k ≥ 3 share means the landscape is genuinely higher-order.

Tensegrity

Thin teal lines are cables (tension), thick dark lines are struts (compression), squares are pinned sites. A straight line of bonds between pinned ends can carry tension on its own, so the plain kagome supports a uniform prestress. The mechanical twist bends those lines and forces a mix of cables and struts. In the sheaf reading, floppy modes are H⁰ and self-stresses H¹ of the rigidity sheaf.

Sheaf cohomology

For SO(2) stalks, a connected component carries a 2-dimensional space of global sections exactly when every cycle's holonomy is the identity. Otherwise it carries none. Computed exactly from a spanning tree.
