The three equations

ẋ = A(a) x + B(a) u
W(T) = ∫0T eAτ B B⊤ eA⊤τ dτ
E* = d⊤W−1d,  d = xT − eATx0

x is the deviation from a healthy reference (the origin). A is what the body does on its own: the diagonal is repair (−k₁, −k₂), the off-diagonal is one problem feeding another. Each column of B is one drug's push in state space. d is the correction treatment has to supply on top of what repair already does, which is why the ellipse is centred on the untreated endpoint and not on the start.

Things to try

  • Weaker repair, bigger Gramian. Slide age up. The smallest eigenvalue of W rises and restoration still gets harder. Weak repair lets a drug push further, and it also leaves more damage for the drug to remove. W only measures movement relative to the untreated endpoint.
  • One drug, two axes. One channel acts on x₁ only, but x₁ feeds x₂, so [B AB] has rank 2 and both are steerable. Then switch on drug 2 and watch W_new = W_old + W₂ fatten the ellipse.
  • Redundant drugs. Two drugs, nearly parallel pushes. Two names, about one direction. The ellipse is a needle and the cost lives in the hard mode.
  • Terminally damaged. Same body as Young, aged. The violet set no longer contains the target, so V = ∞. Raise the cap or lengthen T and it comes back, so “terminal” here is relative to the toolkit.

What V means here

With the cap off, V = E*, the plain minimum of ∫|u|² dt. With the cap on, V is the minimum of ∫|u|² dt over plans with every |uk(t)| ≤ U that land exactly on the target. That one is solved exactly for this linear model (dual Newton on uk = clip(λ·eA(T−t)bk, ±U)), and it is +∞ when the target sits outside the capped reachable set, which is checked with its support function. The stage chip uses arbitrary bands (0.25 and 1.5) that only make sense inside this toy.

What this toy leaves out

  • The axes are illustrative labels on a 2-D linearisation. Real A(a) and B(a) are unknown, nonlinear and drift along the treatment path.
  • “Energy” is quadratic control effort. Not ATP, not money, not toxicity.
  • The safe-region box is only checked. The plan is not re-optimised to avoid it, so a red segment means “this plan is unacceptable”, not “nothing works”.
  • Gramian eigenvalues depend on the units of x. Rescale an axis and the spectrum changes.
  • Whether ageing really shrinks the smallest eigenvalues is a biological hypothesis. The paradox case shows the maths does not force it.
